- 0.75 l whole milk
- 75g butter
- 0.5 dl plain flour

> Simultaneously:
> - Pot 1: Heat the milk on high. Whisk to prevent burning.
> - Pot 2: Melt the butter.
>
> When the butter is melted, whisk in the flour until lump-free. Add the warm milk gradually until all is incorporated.
> 
> Simmer for 1 minute. Season with salt and pepper.

category: Side Dish
time: 2 h
vegan: true
---

- 3 aubergines

> _This step can be skipped if you buy ready-made roasted aubergine dip._
>
> Light the grill and let the coals turn to embers.
> 
> Prick a few holes in the aubergines with a fork (otherwise they explode!).
> 
> Grill the aubergines whole for 25-30 minutes. The skin should be well charred when done. Turn every 5 minutes.
>
> Remove the aubergines from the grill, cut open and scoop all the flesh into a bowl.

---

- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp tahini
- 1-2 garlic cloves
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- Salt

> Press the garlic into the aubergines and mix together with the remaining ingredients. Blend smooth and taste whether more of anything is needed.

---

- Fresh parsley
- Black olives

> Garnish and serve.

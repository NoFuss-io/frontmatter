- Pork tenderloin (approx. 3 servings/tenderloin)
- Bacon (almost a full pack per tenderloin)
- Provençal herb seasoning, Findus (1/2 pack per tenderloin). If you can't
  get hold of the frozen herbs, mix dried, fresh chopped or frozen herbs.
  Basil, oregano, thyme and rosemary.
- Salt & pepper

> ### Pork Tenderloin
> Get out a roasting pan or oven-safe dish, preheat the oven to 175° and open both the tenderloin and bacon packs (you will get messy).
> 
> Cut the tenderloin lengthways so it can be unrolled into a flat piece.
> 
> Season with salt and pepper and add the herbs.
> 
> Roll up, wrap in the bacon slices and place in the roasting pan.
> 
> Insert a meat thermometer into a tenderloin from the side. The entire thermometer should if possible be inside and the tip should be in the thickest part.
> 
> Roast in the middle of the oven (175°) until the internal temperature is 65-70°, which takes approx. 45 min.
> 
> Let rest approx. 10 min before carving.

- 1.5 tbsp margarine
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 3 dl water
- 1/2 vegetable stock cube
- 0.75 dl cream
- 2 tsp Dijon mustard

> ### Mustard Sauce
> Melt margarine, stir in the flour and remove from heat.
> 
> Add water a little at a time, whisking to keep it smooth.
> 
> Add the stock cube.
> 
> Bring to the boil and simmer for approx. 5 min. Stir occasionally.
> 
> Add the cream and mustard and simmer a little longer. Stir occasionally.
> 
> Taste. Add more mustard, salt or pepper as needed.
> 
> If the sauce is too thin, dissolve a little flour in cold water and add to the sauce while stirring. Simmer for a while.
> 
> If the sauce is too thick, thin with water and adjust seasoning with more salt and pepper as needed.

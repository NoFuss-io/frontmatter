- 1 large ripe banana (200 g)
- 2.4 dl rolled oats (75 g)
- 1 tbsp canola oil
- 10 ml black sesame (or nigella seeds?)
- 20 ml raisins

> Mix and bake in the oven at 175°C for approx. 20 min.

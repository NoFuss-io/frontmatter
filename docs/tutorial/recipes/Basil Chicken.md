- 1 kg potatoes
- 4 carrots
- Butter
- Salt, pepper

> Root vegetable mash: Dice potatoes and carrots and simmer until soft (20 min). Mash and season.

---

- 600 g chicken thigh fillets
- 300 g crème fraîche
- 50 ml soy sauce
- 2 tsp honey
- Fresh basil

> Brown the chicken.
> 
> Lower the heat and add crème fraîche, soy sauce, honey, salt, pepper. Simmer 10 min.
> 
> Roughly chop basil and stir into the sauce.

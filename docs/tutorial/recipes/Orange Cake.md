- 1 orange
- 50g sugar

> ### Candied orange peel (the evening before)
> Cut long strips of orange peel. Make sure to remove all the white pith on the inside and cut them into 5mm wide strips.
> 
> Place the peel in boiling water to just cover. Add half the sugar and boil for 1 minute.
> 
> Pour away the sugar water, mix the peel with the rest of the sugar and let dry in a warm place (preferably overnight).

- 1.5 ml allspice
- 1.5 ml nutmeg
- 0.5 ml cloves
- 0.5 ml coriander
- 0.5 ml ginger

Mix [mixed spice](http://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/181605/mixed-spice) if you can't buy it ready-made.

---

- 1 small orange with thin peel
- 275g flour (approx. 4.5 dl)
- 2 tbsp baking powder
- 275g sugar
- 225g butter
- 4 eggs
- Spices
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp mixed spice (above)

> ### Cake
> Take butter and eggs out of the fridge.
> 
> Wash the orange and boil it whole for 30 minutes (whole orange submerged in boiling water).
> 
> Preheat the oven to 180°C or 160°C fan.
> 
> Grease and flour a 20 cm baking tin.
> Alternatively, use a taller and narrower tin so you can split the cake into layers like a layer cake.
>
> Remove the stalk and blend into a purée.
> 
> Reserve 2 tbsp purée for the icing.
> 
> Wait until the purée has cooled, then mix the batter:
> 
> 1. First mix flour and baking powder.
> 2. Add remaining ingredients except the purée and mix without overworking the batter.
> 3. Finally add the purée and stir in.
> 
> Pour the batter into the tin and bake 30-35 minutes. It's done when it's light golden brown and has pulled slightly away from the tin's edges.
> 
> Let the cake cool in the tin for a few minutes so it releases even more from the edges, then turn out onto a rack to cool.

---

- 50g butter
- 175g powdered sugar
- 250g mascarpone
- 2 tbsp orange purée (from the orange in the cake)

> ### Icing
> Put room temperature butter in a bowl and whisk in the powdered sugar gradually with an electric whisk.
> 
> Add mascarpone and orange purée and whisk together.
> 
> Spread the icing on the cake and decorate with the candied orange peel.

---

> When I've made this cake in England it's turned out great every time. In Sweden however it's always turned out too sweet and cloying.
>
> So try either
>
> - Flavour the orange purée with something bitter, e.g. Campari or Angostura bitters. Add red food colouring for a really vibrant finish! Bergamot oil would also be cool to try, but that might be too many experiments at once.
> - Flavour the icing with something sour, e.g. lime juice.

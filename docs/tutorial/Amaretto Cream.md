- 1 l cream
- 70 cl amaretto

> ### Original instructions
> Whip the cream stiffly (*almost* to butter).
> 
> Add a small amount of amaretto and whip. Add more amaretto gradually until all is incorporated.
> 
> The end result should be a pourable cream (otherwise you can neither pour nor drink it).
> 
> ### My tips
> Don't over-whip! The cream will continue to whip as you add the amaretto and can split.
>
> Stop immediately if you had good consistency and don't quickly regain it. Whipping harder will cause it to split.

---

- 6 cl [[Homemade Berry Liqueur|Berry Liqueur]]
- 1.5 cl lemon juice
- Spice, e.g. long pepper

> ### Serving suggestion
> Shake liqueur and juice with ice.
>
> Strain into a chilled glass.
>
> Top with amaretto cream by holding a spoon just above the drink and pouring carefully into the spoon so it flows over onto the drink.

- 1 carrot, coarsely grated
- 1 yellow onion, finely chopped
- 1-2 celery stalks, finely chopped
- 1-2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- Oil for frying

> Peel and coarsely grate the carrot. Finely chop the celery (not in the original recipe, but a given in a classic bolognese). Peel and chop onion and garlic.
>
> Sauté the mirepoix until soft in a pot. It's good if it can cook for a long time without browning so everything softens — this develops the flavours and especially the sweetness.

---

- 400 g pasta

> Start cooking the pasta.

---

- 1.5 dl black beluga lentils
- 500 g crushed tomatoes
- 2 dl crème fraîche or cream
- Pasta water
- 1 vegetable stock cube
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- A little cumin

> Add the sauce to the mirepoix and let simmer with the lid slightly ajar for 25 minutes, or until the lentils have a good texture.

---

- Basil or oregano
- Black pepper

> Season with dried oregano or fresh basil near the end.
>
> You can then blend the sauce with a hand blender, or let it cook a while longer until the lentils are softer. Season with salt and grind over black pepper.

---

- Parmesan

> Garnish and serve.
